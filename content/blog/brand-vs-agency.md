Woosh is a Nigeria-first marketplace, but the **workspace type** matters. Registering as the wrong seat makes client work and your own campaigns collide.

## Brand

A brand workspace is one organisation, one NGN wallet, and briefs **you** run. Discovery, fund-before-select, and the campaign thread are the same product. You are not meant to park three unrelated clients under one brand login and hope finance can unwind it later.

If that is your company doing UGC or influencer work for itself, start at [for brands](/for-brands).

## Agency

An agency seat is for people who hold a **roster of clients**. Client separation, who can approve spend, and which wallet pays which brief are the reasons the seat exists. Do not treat it as a prettier brand account.

If you pitch and run campaigns for other companies, use [for agencies](/for-agencies).

## What does not change

Claimed socials, NGN on Paystack, and fund-before-select apply in both seats. Creators are paid the agreed rate — Woosh takes 0% platform fee. The campaign still has one thread for drafts and revisions.

The question is only: are you buying for yourself, or buying for clients? Register for the seat you will actually use, then invite teammates instead of sharing a password.
