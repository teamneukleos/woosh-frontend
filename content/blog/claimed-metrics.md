A handle can look right and still be a guess. On Woosh, discovery is built around **claimed** accounts: the creator connects Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, and the numbers you see come from that provider.

## What “claimed” means

Until a creator connects a channel, the profile is not treated as verified supply. Unclaimed or invited handles stay labelled as pending. You can still invite someone who is not on Woosh yet. The handle attaches, but it does not become claimed supply until they join and connect.

That is the point. You are not buying a screenshot of a follower count typed into a form.

## What you can trust in search

Search and shortlists are meant to show connected channels, not a bio someone wrote last night. Metrics refresh from the provider on a schedule. They are not a public rating, and Woosh does not invent engagement scores to make a creator look “top 1%”.

If a channel is missing, it is usually because it is not connected yet. Ask the creator to claim, or send an invite, rather than pasting numbers into a spreadsheet.

## How this changes a brief

When you publish a brief, you are looking at people who can prove the account. Selection still happens after you fund the wallet enough to cover the creator rate. Woosh does not add a platform fee on top. Claimed metrics tell you who is in the market. They do not skip the funded brief.
