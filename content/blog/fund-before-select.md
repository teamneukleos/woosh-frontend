Most Nigerian creator work still breaks at money: a rate in chat, a transfer later, and a finance thread that marketing never sees. Woosh treats selection as a funded commitment.

## Draft is free. Selection is not.

You can write and publish a brief without topping up to the last naira. Creators can apply. Nothing is locked until you **accept**.

Accepting a creator pulls the **agreed rate** from the brand or agency wallet. Woosh takes **0% platform fee**. The campaign cannot pretend the money will appear after the reel goes live. NGN only. Paystack is the rail. USD display is not offered until that rail is real.

## Why this exists

“Pay after post” sounds flexible until the creator has delivered and finance is waiting on a PO. Fund-before-select puts the same number in front of marketing and finance before work starts.

If the wallet cannot cover the commitment, you top up first. You do not select on a promise.

## After the money is committed

The brief is not the last mile. Drafts, revisions, usage and deadlines live on the [campaign thread](/blog/campaign-thread). Release follows delivery and the terms of the campaign, not a separate WhatsApp deal.

Brands: one organisation, one wallet, briefs you actually run. See [for brands](/for-brands). Agencies holding several clients use a different seat — [for agencies](/for-agencies).
