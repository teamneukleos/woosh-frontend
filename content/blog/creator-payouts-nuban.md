If you create in Nigeria, the messy part is rarely the shoot. It is the transfer, the “we will sort it Monday”, and the rate that changed in a voice note. Woosh pays in **NGN** to a **verified NUBAN**.

## Connect, then claim

Your profile is useful when Instagram, TikTok or YouTube is connected. That is how brands see claimed metrics instead of a typed follower count. Packages you publish sit on that claimed profile.

Invites from a brand attach as pending until you join. Claiming is the step that turns a handle into supply.

## Money before you say yes

Before you accept work, you should see the **rate you will be paid**. Woosh takes **0% platform fee**. If the number is not obvious, do not treat a chat rate as the contract. The product is supposed to show the rate on the job, not hide it in a PDF.

Payouts go to the NUBAN you verify. Bank details are not something you paste into a group for the intern to copy.

## What Woosh does not do

There is **no public rating** of creators. Brands are not shopping a leaderboard of stars. Delivery quality lives in the campaign thread: files, revisions, deadlines.
