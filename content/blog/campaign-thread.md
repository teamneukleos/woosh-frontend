A funded selection is not the finish line. The work still has to land: a draft, a note, a revision, a usage line, a date someone can point at. On Woosh that lives on the **campaign**, not a new group chat.

## What belongs on the thread

Creators upload into the same place the brief was accepted. You request a revision without starting “Campaign_Lagos_final”. Usage rights and deadlines are part of the record marketing and finance can both see.

If the file only exists in someone’s camera roll, the campaign is not done. Put it on the thread.

## Why this is slower on purpose

WhatsApp is fast until you need an audit. Woosh is slower in the sense that the ledger is the product: who was selected, what was funded in NGN, what was delivered, what is still open.

Disputes and reminders are meant to hang off that record, not off a forwarded screenshot.
